About

The story behind the sound.

ramiro_8978 is an independent artist project connecting music, visual art and personal storytelling. The musical foundation reaches back to hip-hop and rap — the sound that shaped the early years and also the first tracks created as ramiro_8978.

From there, the sound expanded into electronic music, melodic house, deep house, UK garage and other genre-blending influences. The centre remains the same: emotion, relationships, uncertainty, resilience, questions and lived experience.

RootsHip-hop and rap — the music that shaped the beginning.
EvolutionElectronic music, house, UK garage and atmospheric club sound.
Visual languageBlack, gold, cinematic imagery and the recurring crown of ramiro_8978.
