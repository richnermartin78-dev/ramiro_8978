About

Music. Art. Soul.

ramiro_8978 is an independent artist project with roots in hip-hop and rap and a growing electronic identity. The sound moves through electronic music, melodic house, deep house, UK garage and other genre-blending influences, while the writing remains focused on emotion, relationships, uncertainty, resilience and lived experience.

01

Music

Hip-hop roots, electronic evolution and genre-blending releases shaped around atmosphere, emotion and memorable hooks.

02

Art

Cover artwork and visual concepts that extend each song into a distinct narrative world.

03

Soul

Human themes — questions, relationships, resilience, uncertainty and the tension between darkness and light.

Visual identity

Black. Gold. Cinematic.

The ramiro_8978 visual language uses the recurring gold crown, the artist wordmark and “MUSIC | ART | SOUL” as a consistent master-brand treatment.
